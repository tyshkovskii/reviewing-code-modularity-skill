## Interpretation and limits

The implementation case did not discriminate between the three arms. The model
already handled this small extraction without the skill. The facade case is one
useful restraint check, not a false-positive-rate estimate. Neither result supports
a claim of improved review accuracy, efficiency, or automatic skill activation.

The authoring agents inspected these fixtures. Treat them as development smoke
evidence; do not reuse these observations as an untouched holdout score. A release
claim needs repeated paired trials, independent harder tasks, recorded model and
harness settings, blind quality grading, and cost measurements as described in
`../README.md`.
